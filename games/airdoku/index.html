What makes it fun

  • Boards from 5×5 to 10×10
  • Daily puzzles, an endless campaign and Quick Play
  • Notes, automatic cloud marks, undo and hints

How to play

  1. Place exactly one plane in each colored zone, row and column.
  2. Keep planes apart, including diagonally.
  3. Tap to mark a cloud; double tap to place a plane. Use logic to protect your three hearts.
