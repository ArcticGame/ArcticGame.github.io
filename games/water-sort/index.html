Water Sort

What makes it fun

  • 6,000 solver-checked levels
  • Move targets and up to three stars per level
  • Undo, hints and a spare tube when you need help

How to play

  1. Tap a tube, then another tube to pour the top color.
  2. Pour only onto the same color or into an empty tube with room.
  3. Sort every color into its own tube to clear the level.
