What makes it fun

  • A 32-card deck and ten unit tiers
  • Three-star merges and strategic economy upgrades
  • Fifty rounds, boss fights and unlockable endless play

How to play

  1. Draw five cards and exchange the ones you do not need.
  2. Lock in a poker hand to summon a unit, then merge identical units.
  3. Upgrade your force and keep the enemy count below sixty.
