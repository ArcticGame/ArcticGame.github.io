What makes it fun

  • Hundreds of levels with stacked tile layouts
  • Sixteen illustrated tile faces
  • Undo, hints and progress saved after every move

How to play

  1. Tap an uncovered tile to put it in the seven-slot tray.
  2. Collect three identical tiles to remove them from the tray.
  3. Clear the board before the tray fills without a match.
