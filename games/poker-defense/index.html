What makes it fun

  • Ten tower tiers, from High Card to Royal Flush
  • Merge identical towers up to three stars
  • Bosses every ten rounds and an unlockable endless mode

How to play

  1. Spend tickets to draw five cards, then exchange cards to improve your hand.
  2. Lock in your hand to summon a tower and merge matching towers.
  3. Clear round 50 before sixty enemies build up on the track.
