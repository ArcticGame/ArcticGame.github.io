What makes it fun

  • An 8×8 board with no time limit
  • Multi-line clears, combos and an all-clear bonus
  • Automatic saves so you can return to your run

How to play

  1. Drag one of the three tray pieces onto the board.
  2. Fill a complete row or column to clear it.
  3. Keep space for the next pieces. Your run ends when none of them fit.
