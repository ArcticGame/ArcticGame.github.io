What makes it fun

  • Klondike with draw-one or draw-three rules
  • FreeCell with numbered deals and automatic completion
  • Unlimited undo and hints that explain a move

How to play

  1. Choose Klondike or FreeCell to start a deal.
  2. Build descending columns in alternating colors to uncover and free cards.
  3. Move each suit to its foundation in order, from Ace to King.
